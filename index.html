About Pomonero

Pomonero is a Pomodoro timer app designed for students preparing for exams. It helps you stay focused, track your study sessions, and compete with friends on leaderboards.

Key features include customizable timers, 31 unique achievements, daily/weekly/monthly leaderboards, detailed statistics with calendar view, multiple color themes, and note-taking capabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pomonero?

Pomonero is a free Pomodoro technique timer app that helps students focus on their studies with gamification features, achievements, and social leaderboards.

Is Pomonero free?

Yes, Pomonero is free to download and use. A premium version is available with additional features.

How do I track my study progress?

Go to the Statistics tab to see your daily, weekly, and monthly study data, including total hours, session counts, and streak information.

How do leaderboards work?

Leaderboards show daily, weekly, and monthly rankings based on total study time. Compete with other students to stay motivated.

Is my data safe?

Yes, your data is securely stored on encrypted servers. We do not share your personal information with third parties.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, please send an email to pomoonero@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Privacy Policy

Pomonero collects minimal data necessary to provide its services, including study session data and account information. We use Firebase Analytics to improve the app experience. We do not sell your personal data. For questions about privacy, contact us at pomoonero@gmail.com.
